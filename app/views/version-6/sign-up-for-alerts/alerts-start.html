Set up air pollution alerts

Get a free alert by text message or email when air pollution is predicted to be high, or if a nearby monitoring site measures high pollution levels.

You can choose to get alerts about overall pollution levels, or alerts about specific pollutants:

  • nitrogen dioxide
  • PM2.5
  • PM10
  • ozone
  • sulphur dioxide

You can also choose the pollution levels you want to get alerts for – moderate, high or very high.

The alerts include health advice and what you can do to reduce your exposure. You can change or stop your alerts at any time.
